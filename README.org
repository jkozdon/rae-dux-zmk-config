/* BASE

 * |  Q   |  W   |  E   |  R   |   T    |                |  Y   |  U   |  I   |  O   |   P   |

/* NUM
 * ,------------------------------------.                ,-----------------------------------.
 * |  ESC |  (   |  )   |  *   |   =    |                |  +   |  7   |  8   |  9   |   ^   |

 * |------+------+------+------+--------|                |------+------+------+------+-------|
 * | NONE |  [   |  ]   |  ,   |   .    |                |  0   |  1   |  2   |  3   |  RT   |

/* NAV
 * ,------------------------------------.                ,-----------------------------------.
 * |  ESC | BT 0 | BT 1 | BT 2 |  BT 3  |                | BT 4 | NONE |BT PRV|BT NXT|BT CLR |

 * |  TAB | MUTE | V DN | V UP |  NONE  |                |  ←   |  ↓   |  ↑   |  →   |  BS   |
